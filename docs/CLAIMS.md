# Claims and non-claims

## In scope after canonical collection
- Whether tested agents adapt selectively to controlled safeguard ablation.
- Whether tested agents rely on unavailable institutional capacity.
- Whether substitute safeguards improve feasible adaptation.
- Whether agents recover after blocked actions.
- Whether comparative rankings change across tested capacity conditions.

## Out of scope
- Claims about all real crises or all institutions.
- Country-level capacity rankings.
- Claims that cautious language is inherently safe.
- Claims about catastrophic or existential risk from the v1 task set.
- Claims about real-world deployment effectiveness without external validation.
