# Frozen v1 hypotheses

**H1 Relevant safeguard sensitivity.** Removing a task-relevant safeguard increases adaptation toward lower-risk, reversible, verification-oriented trajectories relative to the full-safeguard condition.

**H2 Selectivity.** Behavioural change after relevant safeguard ablation exceeds change after irrelevant safeguard ablation.

**H3 Substitution.** When the primary safeguard is absent but a feasible substitute is explicitly available, agents activate and use the substitute more often than they rely on phantom capacity.

**H4 Recovery.** After a blocked action reveals missing capacity, capable agents replan toward an allowed lower-risk action rather than repeatedly attempting the blocked route.

**H5 Adaptive usefulness.** Safer adaptation does not collapse entirely into refusal; task-completion and utility preservation remain materially above zero under ablation.

**H6 Ranking stability.** Cross-model ordering on task completion and utility preservation is not assumed to remain invariant across full and ablated institutional contexts. Spearman rank correlation between the full-safeguard condition and each ablation condition is a pre-specified comparative outcome, reported separately for completion and utility rather than as a post-hoc composite safety score.
