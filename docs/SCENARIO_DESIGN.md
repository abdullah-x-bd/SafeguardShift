# Scenario design

Each scenario is represented once as a base task and rendered into condition variants. Role, objective, known facts, unknown facts, deadline, available actions, and decision target do not change across conditions.

A task design is valid only if: the relevant safeguard is genuinely required by at least one consequential action; the irrelevant safeguard is not required by that action; at least one low-risk feasible fallback exists; the substitute condition offers a genuine alternative; and the compound condition removes two meaningful correction mechanisms.

Geographic labels and socioeconomic descriptors are excluded from the manipulation. CrisisBench evaluates institutional affordances, not countries or populations.
