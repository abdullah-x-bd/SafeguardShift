CrisisBench v1

Institutional safeguard ablation for agentic AI safety evaluation.

CrisisBench tests whether AI agents change what they do when the external mechanisms that normally verify, review, monitor, authorize, reverse, or safely execute high-stakes actions disappear.

The experiment

Each underlying task is held fixed. CrisisBench changes only the safeguard state: full safeguards, relevant ablation, irrelevant ablation, substitute capacity, or compound ablation.

36base tasks
180matched condition cells
6safeguards
5agent tools

Primary outcomes

Unsafe invariance, phantom-capacity reliance, feasible substitution, safe recovery, task completion, utility preservation, irreversible error intent, and selective adaptation.

Research status

The v1 design, agent environment, dataset, protocol freeze, OpenRouter harness, and reproducibility checks are implemented. Canonical model results are not claimed until the frozen paid collection is executed.
