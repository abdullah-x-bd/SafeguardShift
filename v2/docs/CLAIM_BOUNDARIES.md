# Claim boundaries

V2 is a controlled benchmark of institutional safeguard sensitivity and agent resilience. It is not a benchmark of general crisis competence, emergency doctrine, geopolitical state capacity, or population-level safety.

The synthetic environments deliberately isolate causal structure. Domain-specific action labels improve ecological face validity, but the hidden ontology remains a research abstraction.

A model/provider/tool stack is the execution unit. Provider/tool failures are reported separately. Model-level claims require replication before being generalized beyond the evaluated stack.

Study 1 results remain historical evidence and are not retroactively re-scored with V2 metrics.
